History

Notes on history. I put some pins in the map to events I have read about. This is related to my books section.

The continents cannot be reliably placed before about 1 billion years ago.

Borders from 1886 to 2019 are year by year, from CShapes 2.0 (ETH Zurich, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, simplified). Earlier borders are approximate snapshots from historical-basemaps (GPL-3.0), starting at 10,000 BCE. Before that, the map shows today's coastlines moved to where plate reconstructions place them (GPlates Web Service, model of Merdith et al. 2021, CC BY 4.0), not the actual shorelines of the time; geological periods follow the International Chronostratigraphic Chart.
