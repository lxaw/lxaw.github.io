What is the value of writing in today's world? Why bother putting pen to paper when you can give a few keywords, a general gist of an idea, and then let the machine put it all together?

That's all good and fun, but I think we should not yet give up on writing as a form of expression. The spoken word has many constraints; we must speak when spoken to, in a time frame such as to not frustrate the other party, while always considering the time, place, and people of the discussion itself. What we may truly believe or intend to say is oftentimes obfuscated by the outside.

Writing does not have such constraints. We have as much time as we want, and do not have to care about another person's evaluation or the time of day. We can write as much or as little as we want, about whatever we want, using whatever words we want in whatever language we choose. It is a freeing endeavor that allows us to discretize using our own tokenizer of choice the continuity of thoughts in our minds.

It is in this way that I believe that writing is the freest and most intimate form of expression. It is the closest way that we can draw another person into our mind and let them see how we structure our thoughts.
