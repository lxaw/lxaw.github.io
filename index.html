Lexington Whalen

Research Scientist, NVIDIA Research (DLER)  ·  NSF Graduate Research Fellow, Georgia Tech

I am a Research Scientist on the DLER team at NVIDIA Research in Santa Clara, CA. Previously, at SoftBank's SB Intuitions, I co-led pretraining and inference optimization for the Sarashina LLM family — Japan's largest language models. Before that, during my first stint at NVIDIA Research, I co-developed Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion (EMNLP 2026) and co-led Efficient-DLM (ICML 2026). I was an NSF Graduate Research Fellow and pursued an M.S. in CS at Georgia Tech. I also serve as an informal advisor on U.S.–Japan AI policy, joining periodic roundtables with U.S. Embassy Tokyo.

I am a history enthusiast and enjoy discussing history, meaning, and geopolitics. I also enjoy climbing and bouldering, and am trying to get better at it. There's other stuff too probably.

I am committed to mentoring students, and make time for this every week. Please feel free to message me on LinkedIn and I will try to respond. Any background is welcome, and the request can be anything from resume reviews to just general advice. Please note that I cannot help with internship / university / job applications unless I directly was involved in your project.
