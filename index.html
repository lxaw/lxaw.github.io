Lexington Whalen

NVIDIA Research

I am a Research Scientist on the DLER team in Santa Clara, CA. Previously, at SoftBank's SB Intuitions, I co-led pretraining and inference optimization for the Sarashina LLM family — Japan's largest language models. Before that, during my first stint at NVIDIA Research, I co-developed Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion (EMNLP 2026) and co-led Efficient-DLM (ICML 2026). I was an NSF Graduate Research Fellow and pursued an M.S. in CS at Georgia Tech. I also serve as an informal advisor to government officials, and have previously worked with representatives in California and embassy members in Japan.

Note: I can only advise on topics that are public knowledge, and can only give my opinion based on publicly available materials.

I am a history enthusiast and enjoy discussing history, meaning, and geopolitics. I also enjoy climbing and bouldering, and am trying to get better at it. There's other stuff too probably.
